Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

This page explains what information I collect through my "What's actually holding back your results?" quiz, why I collect it, and how it's looked after. If anything here isn't clear, just get in touch, contact details are at the bottom of this page.

What I collect

When you take the quiz and submit your details, I collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your Instagram handle
  • Your email address
  • Your quiz answers and result

Why I collect it

I use this information to:

  • Send you your personalised quiz result
  • Follow up with relevant next steps based on your result
  • Send you occasional tips, offers, and updates from Coached by Taylor, if you've opted in to receive these

Where it's stored

Your details are stored securely in Brevo, the email platform I use to manage my mailing list and send emails. Brevo is GDPR-compliant and does not sell or share your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How long I keep it

I keep your details on file for as long as you remain subscribed to my mailing list. If you unsubscribe, or ask me to delete your information, it will be removed.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • Tell you what information I hold about you
  • Correct anything that's inaccurate
  • Delete your information entirely

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link at the bottom of any email I send. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any other time, just contact me directly using the details below.
